# Reviewed final report notes for Dolly's assistant

Source: user-supplied final Dolly Group Project report, sections 3–6 (Figures 2–5). This is a selected summary of the team's reported findings; no analyses were rerun. The report does not provide a final leaderboard score or rank. It calls Dolly v1 an intermediate development model; the actual 7 October leaderboard submission used the separate XGBoost pipeline described in S3.

## Model and evaluation

- Dolly v2 averages three LightGBM fits and combines per-read scores (up to 20 reads per site), 5-mer-centred signal summaries and 7-mer sequence, neighbouring sites within 25 and 100 nt, and 16 Ensembl 91 transcript features. Read and neighbour scores for training were generated out of fold. Transcripts without annotation use the v1 fallback.
- Development used five-fold gene-grouped CV on dataset0, with 775 genes held out. Dataset1 tested 15,137 sites on unseen transcripts in a second sequencing run. Dataset2 tested how scores track known synthetic modification fractions. The report used 1,000 gene bootstrap resamples for 95% intervals.
- On held-out dataset0 genes, v2 ROC/PR AUC was 0.952/0.561 (PR 95% CI 0.505–0.619), compared with m6Anet PR AUC 0.485 and the 5-mer baseline 0.142. On dataset1 unseen transcripts, v2 was 0.877/0.434 vs m6Anet PR AUC 0.348. The paired PR gain over v1 on dataset1 was 0.032 (95% CI 0.015–0.049). These are study metrics, not leaderboard results.
- Transcript position was the largest CV improvement (+0.055 PR AUC); read-level scores and signal summaries accounted for most of the fitted model's feature gain. Dataset2 within-5-mer Spearman correlation was 0.64 for Dolly and 0.69 for m6Anet. Scores are not estimates of the fraction of modified molecules.

## SG-NEx findings and interpretation

- The report scored 23 runs (2,973,240 site observations at 20+ reads). MCF7 replicate 3 used the v1 fallback because of versioned transcript IDs; cell-line comparisons excluded it, leaving 22 runs and 442,435 unique sites. SG-NEx has no direct labels: the evaluation matched sites to held-out Hct116 dataset1 labels on genes absent from training (12,279 eligible sites), yielding median run ROC/PR AUC of about 0.86/0.39. This does not independently validate modification status in each cell line.
- On matched labels, observed precision levelled off around 50% for scores above 0.5. A cutoff of 0.5 gave about 35% recall; 0.9 gave about 0.5% with no precision gain. The score is not a calibrated certainty or modification fraction.
- In the final report's 22-run landscape, 44.7% of calls versus 21.9% of candidates were within 200 nt of the stop codon; 0.4% of calls versus 31.7% of candidates were within 50 nt of a junction. The v1-scored MCF7 run showed a similar pattern without position features. This supports the interpretation but a single fallback run cannot establish what causes model disagreement.
- Of 58,004 sites measured in all seven lines, 2,143 were called in at least one and 949 (412 genes) in all seven, using cell-line mean score ≥0.5. A permutation analysis found four sharply separated candidates against 0.8 expected by chance; these are exploratory, not validated cell-line-specific sites. Raw call rates track run size, and higher Hct116 scores may reflect training-line calibration rather than biology.
- The report describes a downloadable atlas of 11,349 reproducibly called sites in 3,121 genes. Use S1 and S7 for current website behaviour and limitations. Keep the report's analysis populations distinct from the historical comparison in S5 and current dashboard views in S7.

Limitations and future work: new-run performance falls, labels may be noisy or incomplete, training used one cell line, and m6Anet pretraining may overlap Hct116 reads. The report treats available runs as technical replicates for this comparison; they do not establish biological replication. Suggested next steps include re-scoring MCF7 replicate 3 with unversioned IDs, obtaining labels from more lines and biological replicates, and modelling modification fractions directly.
